Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, customizing and planning your trip with AdmireDworld Travel.

How do I book a trip with AdmireDworld Travel?

Choose an India package, international package, fixed departure, or build a fully customized itinerary. Log in with your name, phone number and email (verified by a one-time OTP sent to your email), select your travel dates and number of travellers, and confirm your booking. Our team takes care of the rest.

What is the difference between a fixed departure and a customized package?

A fixed departure is a group tour that leaves on a set date with guaranteed seats. A customized package is built just for you — you choose the destination, duration, budget and travel style, and our team sends a personalized itinerary within 24 hours.

How does the Refer & Earn program work?

Once you're approved as a referral partner, share your personal referral link. When someone signs up through it, they get an instant ₹500 welcome bonus. Once their trip booking is confirmed, you earn your commission percentage of that booking amount as travel credit.

Does AdmireDworld Travel plan destination weddings?

Yes. AdmireDworld Travel plans destination weddings end to end — venue selection, decor, guest travel and on-ground coordination — across curated venues in India and abroad.

Can I view my bookings from any device?

Yes. Log in with the same account (email) on any phone or computer and all your bookings appear, since they are saved against your account on our server rather than on a single device.
